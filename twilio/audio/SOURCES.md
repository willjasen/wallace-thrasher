# Ambient sound sources

Every deployed recording must have a verifiable license that permits use in
this project, including commercial use. Do not add CC BY-NC, editorial-only,
personal-use-only, unidentified, or otherwise incompatible recordings. CC BY
material must retain complete attribution. If a source or license can no longer
be verified, remove the recording from the project and from Twilio.

The current deployed renditions are 2.5-second, 16 kHz mono PCM WAV files at
256 kbps. They were regenerated from the higher-quality source renditions listed
below instead of being upsampled from the former 16 kHz MP3 call assets. These
are call assets, not archival masters. Preserve source-quality lossless masters
at their native sample rates when available, and use a higher-quality lossless
Twilio-supported rendition when a verified wideband or HQ call path benefits
from it. Never manufacture supposed HQ audio by upsampling a lower-resolution
file. New ambient cues should normally be 2–3 seconds long.

The deployed clips are telephone-optimized excerpts of Mixkit sound effects,
used under the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License.

- `forklift-beep-v2.wav`: Truck reversing beeps loop (Mixkit SFX 1077)
- `distant-truck-v2.wav`: Truck in highway passing by (Mixkit SFX 1627)
- `paper-handling-v3.wav`: Papers being moved or wrinkling (Mixkit SFX 2379)

Mixkit states that its sound effects may be used in commercial and personal
projects without required attribution. License and source library:

- https://mixkit.co/license/
- https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/
